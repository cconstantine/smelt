# Feature checklist: Conversations and the chat page
Part of the [Feature checklist](index.md).

Conversations, the transcript, replies, stopping, questions and tabs.

| Ticket | Feature | How to check in the browser |
| -- | -- | -- |
| SME-5 | Delete conversations | Two-click Delete in the sidebar (button keeps its width when armed); deleting the open conversation returns to `/`; a link to a deleted conversation says it doesn't exist |
| SME-6 | History persists | Restart the server; the conversation is intact |
| SME-18 | Context meter, auto-compaction | Meter and detail view; with a small context window set on the conversation's model at `/providers` (e.g. 28000), a turn compacts: the divider appears live in every tab and the model answers the message that triggered it |
| SME-25 | System prompt | Detail view shows the prompt and the environment (right model name); the model knows today's date |
| SME-26 | Sandboxes page, stopping a turn | Sidebar dot; "Sandboxes" (`/pods`) with live usage; two-click Stop; Stop a turn from a second tab (both show "Stopped."); a terminal command finishing after a Stop doesn't start a turn: its notice waits for your next message and shows after it |
| SME-41 | Busy session | With a todo list, a live browser and a sandbox terminal all open at once: at 1400px each side panel is usable (browser and terminal at least 400px wide, the terminal on screen) and the chat keeps room; at phone width the chat comes first and the panels stack below |
| SME-83 | Small scrolls stay put | In a conversation with a reply long enough to scroll, at the bottom, scroll up a little with a trackpad or one wheel notch: the transcript stays where you put it instead of snapping back to the bottom |
| SME-108 | A resize doesn't switch the follow off | With a sandbox terminal streaming a long burst (ask the model to run `seq 1 300` in a terminal): the terminal follows its last line. Drag the window narrower and wider, or zoom, while it runs: it keeps following instead of stopping where the resize left it. Switch to another conversation and back: it opens on its last line. At phone width, repeat through "Conversations". Do the same with a long reply in the transcript. Mid-burst, scroll the terminal up to read, then drag the window taller: the view lands on the bottom once and then stays put while new lines pile up below it — the follow must not restart |
| SME-82 | Context view from the keyboard | In a conversation with a reply, Shift+Tab back from the message box until the context bar is outlined; Enter opens the context view with focus on its × (read as "Close"); Escape closes it and the bar has focus again. Clicking the bar and clicking outside the view still open and close it |
| SME-81 | Errors say what went wrong | On "Sandbox volumes", "+ Add a volume" with mount path `/`: the form says "invalid mount path: a volume can't be mounted over the root directory", with no "error running server function" or "(details: None)" around it. Any other page's error (Git, MCP servers, language servers) reads the same way. Not web-checkable without breaking the server: a failed sidebar action's error goes once a later one (another Delete, New conversation) succeeds (the browser tier's `error_text` scenario fails a Delete on purpose) |
| SME-75 | Text stays still under the pointer while a conversation opens | Open a conversation that has a running sandbox and a long reply, and move the mouse over the transcript at once (try a laptop width and a window under 1024px wide): as the sandbox panel and the context bar appear, the words under the pointer don't move, and clicking a tool row or divider straight away opens that one. Move the pointer off the transcript: it catches up to its latest message |
| SME-34 | The model asks the user (`ask_user`) | Ask the model something it can't decide alone, e.g. "Clean up the build, but ask me first whether to delete the build directory and which targets to build" (with a model that calls tools; repeat the ask more explicitly if it asks in plain text). A card appears below the transcript with each question, its options and a field for your own answer; the sidebar marks the conversation with a "?" and the message box says "Answer the question above, or write a reply instead". Open the conversation in a second tab: the card is there too, and still there after a reload. Choose options (a multi-select question toggles) and Submit: the card goes from both tabs, the call becomes a compact "Asked" row with each answer, the mark clears, and the model carries on using your answer. Ask again and write a message instead of answering: the card goes and the model is told you didn't answer and gets your message. |
| SME-91 | A deleted conversation in another tab; Stop's leftovers | Open a conversation in two tabs and delete it in one: within a few seconds the other shows only "This conversation doesn't exist. It may have been deleted." (no message box, no example asks, no transcript or context bar under it), its sidebar drops the conversation (SME-122 B44), and it stays that way after a reload. Stop a turn whose reply is streaming: the partial reply disappears in every tab at once and "Stopped." appears once. A turn that fails mid-reply leaves no partial text either, even with a message queued behind it (SME-179 B28). Not web-checkable: the races behind it (a notice, message or stop note saved twice, an older turn's error kept after a newer turn, event channels and turn locks kept for good; and SME-179's: Stop hidden as one turn ends and the next starts, a Stop the moment a turn starts, a wake clearing the kept error, turn state kept after a late notice; turn state kept after a Stop or a "Work on a repo" error on a deleted conversation, SME-219; a Stop right after a send or an answer, or as a wake or a delivery is let in, SME-216 and SME-217), covered by tests in `src/api/chat.rs`, `src/api/git.rs`, `src/db.rs`, `src/events.rs`, `src/turn/tests/lifecycle.rs` and `src/turn/tests/stop_gaps.rs` |
| SME-43 | A tab older than the server | Not with a check server: `dx serve` reloads every open tab itself when its server restarts, so no tab is ever stale there. Instead, `dx build --platform web` two commits that differ in `src/` or `assets/` and copy each `target/dx/smelt/debug/web` aside; run the older one's `./server` (with `PORT`, a scratch `DATABASE_URL` and another `SMELT_PREVIEW_ADDR`; both commits must have SME-115's fix: an older server on a scratch database deletes the dev server's sandbox claims), open a conversation and the Sandboxes page, then stop it and run the newer one on the same port. Within a few seconds both tabs show "smelt was updated. Reload to keep this page current." at the top (below "Conversations" at phone width) without reloading, and Reload clears it. The same commit run again shows no banner. Not web-checkable: an event type the bundle doesn't know is skipped without a reconnect (the browser tier's `stale_bundle` scenario) |
| SME-30 | Markdown in replies | Ask the model for a reply with a heading, a bulleted and a numbered list, a task list, a table, a quote, a link and a fenced code block with a language (e.g. "Show me a Rust hello world in a code block, a two-row table of its files, and a task list of next steps"). While it streams, the code block shows as plain text until its closing fence arrives, then highlights; the reply shows formatted, not as `#`, `*` or `|` characters. The code block's Copy button says "Copied" and the clipboard holds the code. The link opens in a new tab. Ask it to reply with `<b>hi</b><script>alert(1)</script>` written as raw HTML: it shows as text, nothing runs. At phone width a wide table or long code line scrolls inside its box, not the page. Switch the system to dark mode: code colours change with it. Scroll to the bottom and ask for an image by URL (e.g. `![x](https://picsum.photos/300/400)`): the transcript stays at the bottom once it loads |
| SME-105 | Copy a whole reply as markdown | Open smelt at its plain-HTTP LAN address (e.g. `http://ryzen.lan:8180`). Ask "Check my todo list, then show me a two-row table and a Python code block": while the reply streams, no "Copy reply" button shows. Once it finishes, its last bubble has "Copy reply (2 parts)" left of the timestamp; pointing at it outlines both the sentence before the "Checked the todo list" row and the final answer, nothing else. Click it: "Copied" for a moment, the outline stays while it shows, then the label goes back. Paste into a text editor: the exact markdown, `##`, `|` and the ```` ```python ```` fence included, the two parts separated by a blank line, no tool output. An earlier reply's button still works while a new reply streams. At phone width the button is finger-sized and a tap outlines what it copied. Dark mode: the outline and button read clearly |
| SME-104 | Bare URLs in replies are links | Ask the model to "Reply with these exactly, no markdown links: `https://example.com/a, www.example.org, me@example.com`, and `https://example.com/code` in backticks" (the first three without backticks). While it streams, each address becomes a link once it arrives. The first three are links whose words are unchanged: the first opens `https://example.com/a` in a new tab, the second `https://www.example.org`, the third starts a mail to `me@example.com`; the trailing comma isn't part of a link. The one in backticks stays code, not a link. Ask it to mention `main.rs` and `example.com`: neither is a link. At phone width and in dark mode the links read like other reply links. Automated: `markdown` unit tests and the browser tier's `markdown_bare_urls` and `markdown_bare_urls_streaming` |
| SME-174 | Deeply nested code doesn't kill the tab | Ask the model: "Reply with a ```` ```d ```` code block holding `$(` written 1,000 times on one line (generate it with a terminal command if you need to), then the word DONE after the block" (model). The block shows highlighted and DONE shows below it. Reload the page: it loads again the same way, and you can still type and send a message. Automated: `highlight` unit tests and the browser tier's `markdown_deep_code` |
| SME-170 | A reply written instead of answering still dismisses the question when a Stop catches it queued | Not web-checkable on demand (the model has to pair `ask_user` with a slow tool, and you have to send and Stop inside that window). If it happens: the card and the sidebar's "?" go from every tab, and your next message's reply shows the model got your text as the reply to its question, with no second "didn't answer". A web-checkable shadow: the SME-34 row's "write a message instead" step. Automated: `turn::tests::ask_user::test_a_written_answer_caught_queued_by_a_stop_dismisses_the_question`, `test_a_notice_caught_queued_by_a_stop_leaves_the_question_waiting` and `test_an_answer_recorded_before_the_stop_goes_with_the_stopped_message` |
| SME-185 | Chat page lows: a failed send, the context view's error, switching | **Failed send:** in a conversation, take the server down (or go offline in the browser's dev tools), send `hello once`: no bubble stays, the text is back in the message box, and the line under the transcript says "Couldn't send the message: …"; bring it back and press Enter: one `hello once`. **Context view:** with the browser offline, click the context bar: the view says "Couldn't load the context: …" instead of "Loading…". **Switching:** switch between two conversations with messages, at 1400px and on a phone through the folded list: the "What should smelt work on?" start screen never flashes up on the way; an empty conversation still shows it. Delete the conversations afterwards. Automated: browser scenario `switch_shows_no_start_screen` |
| SME-202 | Messages a Stop catches queued are saved in the order they were sent | Not web-checkable on demand (the windows are a turn task's spawn latency and the wake order after one Stop, which no UI step can force). If you send two messages while a turn runs and press Stop: both appear in the order you sent them, and "Stopped." once. Automated: `turn::tests::queue_order::test_two_sends_a_stop_catches_queued_are_saved_in_order`, `test_a_stop_arm_running_out_of_order_keeps_send_order`, `test_turns_admitted_back_to_back_queue_in_admission_order`, `test_a_turn_dropped_before_it_runs_frees_its_place`, `test_a_send_aborted_while_queued_leaves_the_queue` and `test_a_send_aborted_once_granted_but_unpolled_frees_the_lock` |
